ABC new-member pull — admin

ABC bills per API call. Nothing is called unless the master switch is on and a club is enabled. Each location has its own on/off toggle and frequency: Daily ≈ 30 calls a month, Weekly ≈ 4, Monthly = 1 (more only if a club has over 5,000 new members in the window). Less frequent pulls are cheaper but in-club joins show up later.

How matching works. A lead is a website form submission (Contact, Charter, Free Trial, Book a Tour) or an iCart checkout started on woat.org / ACH (kiosk excluded). It counts as joined online when the same email completes an iCart checkout within the window, and joined in club when the same email joins on the kiosk, or when an ABC member who joined within the window matches by email, then phone, then name + club. A match shows that the lead later joined; it does not prove the lead caused the join. Not checked yet means ABC has not been read for that club and date. ABC contact details are stored only as salted hashes.
