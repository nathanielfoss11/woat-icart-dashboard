How CPA is calculated

Blended cost per acquisition = $26,849.67 total August spend across all three channels, divided by 3,025 completed memberships across every location in the corporate campaign (kiosk excluded) = $8.88 per join. This is a blended figure — total spend over total joins — not a per-channel attributed CPA, since individual joins are not tied back to a specific ad channel. Including kiosk joins (3,351) would lower it to $8.01.

How these numbers are counted

Member figures are deduplicated to one record per person (by email; a completed join outranks an incomplete one), matching the methodology of the live dashboard at reports.woat.org. Date range is Aug 1–31, 2026 in UTC. Completed = status “joined”; Incomplete = entered checkout, swept after 15 min without completing. Kiosk excluded: all records that came through the kiosk server (platform = kiosk) or carry a Kiosk-named plan are removed. Eulerity excluded: any join whose source parameter (utm_source) is the Eulerity ad platform is also removed — Eulerity is a separate agency running a subset of locations, so its 98 joins are not part of this campaign’s numbers. Because of these two exclusions these totals run below the live reports.woat.org dashboard, which still includes both.

Sources & caveats

Traffic: GA4 “Workout Anytime – Main GA4” property, Total/Active/New users. Memberships: iCart Supabase database powering reports.woat.org. Traffic and membership figures come from different systems and are not expected to tie out 1:1. A small number of joins (10) have no club captured and appear as “Unmapped.”
